About Us

Our Story

Hope City Church started in 2015 with a simple dream: to create a church where everyone belongs. What began as a small gathering of 30 people in a living room has grown into a community of hundreds across Austin.

We're not a perfect church—we're a church full of imperfect people learning to follow Jesus together. We believe that real life change happens in the context of authentic relationships, relevant teaching, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time, returning after years away, or looking for a new church home, we'd love to meet you. You belong here.

Our Mission

To help people find and follow Jesus.

Our Vision

To be a community where everyone experiences the hope, love, and life-changing power of Jesus Christ.

What We Value

Our Core Values

1

Everyone Belongs

No matter your background, you have a place here. We're all on this journey together.

2

Authenticity Matters

We don't pretend to have it all together. We create space for real questions and honest conversations.

3

Love in Action

Faith isn't just about what we believe—it's about how we live. We serve our neighbors and city.

4

Growth is a Process

We're patient with each other. Spiritual growth takes time, and every step forward matters.

Our Faith

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God, the ultimate authority for faith and life.

2 Timothy 3:16-17
God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Matthew 28:19
Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is the Son of God, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died for our sins, and rose again.

John 3:16
Salvation

We believe salvation is a gift from God, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

Ephesians 2:8-9
The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to worship, serve, and share the gospel with the world.

1 Corinthians 12:27
Eternity

We believe in the resurrection of both the saved and the lost—the saved to eternal life, the lost to eternal separation from God.

John 5:28-29
